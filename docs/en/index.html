Offline field app · humanitarian demining and UXO clearance

Stop spending your evenings on forms and hand‑drawn sketches.

After a day in the lanes, team leaders copy the field notebook into daily reports and draw progress on millimetre paper, often for hours. With Terenska knjiga the team records lanes, boxes and finds on site, without signal, and the official forms and the true-scale progress sketch are built from those same records.

§ 01Who it is for

For the team leaders who sign the daily report

Used on site by team and section leaders. The office receives finished documents instead of field notebooks to transcribe.

Commercial

Clearance companies

Accredited firms working BHMAC or CZRS tasks, where the team leader writes the paperwork and the owner answers for deadlines and completion records.

Humanitarian

NGOs and national operators

Teams that need complete, consistent records for the mine action centre, every working day, on every task.

International

Operators in many countries

One app across programmes. Each country's rules and forms come from a country pack, so headquarters can standardise the tool without flattening national requirements.

§ 02How it works

Four steps, one set of records

Everything is entered once, in the field. Documents are built from the same data, so the daily report, the weekly report and the completion record agree with each other.

  1. 01

    Dossier

    Enter the task once: task ID, MSP and CI/SI, turning points, the zero point and azimuth (BiH) or the corner of the box grid (Serbia), and the team.

  2. 02

    Lanes or boxes

    The app lays out the work: 1 m working lanes from the zero line, or a grid of boxes (for example 10 × 10 m) over the area. Every lane and box has a number and a state.

  3. 03

    Field entry

    During the day: time in the lane, the detector test, progress by metre or by box, and finds with type, depth, condition and photo. Large controls; coordinates are never typed.

  4. 04

    Official documents

    At the end of the day the forms are already filled in from those records. Open, check, print or save as PDF. Data also exports as CSV and GeoJSON for QGIS or ArcGIS.

§ 03Country packs

One app, the rulebook of each country

A country pack holds a national standard: work method, coordinate system, item lists, safety distances, emergency numbers, document layouts and the checks the app runs. A site set to another pack gets that country's forms; the app stays the same.

Bosnia and Herzegovina

BA-BHMAC-2024

Built on the BHMAC Standard for mine action in BiH, April 2024 edition, and its annexes (Prilog).

Work method
1 m working lanes from the zero point and azimuth; finds measured by lane and metre.
Coordinates
Gauss-Krüger zone 6 or UTM, one system per document, never mixed.

Documents generated

  • P9
    Daily reportDnevni izvještaj · time log, methods, area cleared, finds
  • P11
    Weekly reportSedmični izvještaj · deadline Monday 16:00 tracked
  • P13
    Technical report on explosive ordnanceStručni izvještaj o ES · with photo documentation
  • P6
    Completion record (draft)Zapisnik o izvršenim aktivnostima TI i čišćenja · annexes and map of the cleared area with turning points and finds; missing fields are flagged
  • P12
    Initial incident/accident reportInicijalni izvještaj · 6-hour deadline countdown

Checks while you work

  • Time in the lane: 30 minutes per shift at most
  • 25 m between active lanes (pairs)
  • Internal QC on at least 3% of the cleared area
  • Internal monitoring at least every 5 working days
  • Detector test on the test piece logged each day

Serbia

RS-CZRS

Built on the Regulation on protection from unexploded ordnance (Uredba, Official Gazette RS 70/2013), IMAS, and how CZRS projects are run.

Work method
Box grid (for example 10 × 10 m). Each box is cleared with CEIA, and with Schonstedt where the project requires it, for example along a railway.
Finds
Measured with a tape from the box corner stake.
Coordinates
SRB_ETRS89 / UTM 34N.

Documents generated

  • SK
    Progress sketchSkica napredovanja · drawn automatically at true scale on millimetre paper, typically 1:1000 (A4, print without scaling)
  • DN
    Daily output per deminerDnevna evidencija učinka · per deminer and method, with the CZRS norm check of 150 m² per deminer per day
  • NUS
    Record of UXO foundZapisnik o pronađenim NUS · with coordinates
  • PR
    Handover record of cleared area (draft)Zapisnik o predaji očišćene površine · basis for the CZRS certificate

CZRS form layouts are not published. The Serbian documents follow Uredba 70/2013 and are matched to the forms each pilot operator actually submits.

More countries are added as packs, built together with the operators who work there.

§ 04Made for the field

Works where the work is

Minefields rarely have signal, and team leaders wear gloves. The app is designed for that, not for a desk.

No signal needed

Installs on the home screen of a phone or tablet. Opens and saves records with no network at all.

Works with gloves

Large buttons and pick-from-list entry instead of typing. Lane numbers, item types and actions are one tap each.

Readable in sunlight

High-contrast light theme and large figures. Red is used only for hazard: finds, incidents, overruns.

Coordinates without GPS

Positions are calculated from the surveyed zero point and azimuth, or the box corner, plus the measured distance. Not from a phone GPS that drifts by metres.

Incident procedure

One button stops work, starts the clock, walks the team through the steps and, in BiH, fills in Prilog 12 against the 6-hour deadline.

Photos as evidence

Find photos are kept at full size on the device and laid out in the photo documentation, two per row, ready to print.

§ 05Data security

Where your data is, as of today

Minefield locations are sensitive. Here is exactly what the app does now, and what it does not do yet.

Today

  • On the device only. Records and photos are stored in the device's local database (IndexedDB). The app asks the system to protect them from automatic clean-up.
  • Nothing goes to a server. Terenska knjiga has no server today; records are not sent anywhere.
  • Backup file. One file with all sites, records and photos, made from inside the app and saved wherever your organisation decides. It restores on another device.
  • The backup file is not encrypted. Keep it with your other task records, and use screen locks on field devices.

Not yet

  • Office sync: in development with pilot organisations, not available today. The "sent" indicator in the demo is a simulation.
  • User accounts and roles: not available yet.
  • Encryption and server location (EU, or your organisation's own server) will be agreed with pilot organisations as a condition for introducing sync.

What Terenska knjiga is not

  • Not a safety decision tool. All safety decisions stay with your SOP, your team leader and the mine action centre.
  • No AI identification of ordnance. The deminer chooses the item type from the country pack's list.
  • Accreditation, the centre's quality control and external monitoring remain exactly as they are.

We ask from you
